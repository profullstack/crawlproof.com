# Trademark and branding

The source in this repository is licensed under the AGPL-3.0 (see
[LICENSE](./LICENSE)). **That licence covers the code. It does not grant any
right to the CrawlProof name, logo, or branding.**

This distinction is normal for open-source projects with a hosted service
behind them, and it exists to protect users rather than to restrict you: a
fork carrying the original name is indistinguishable from the original to
somebody who lands on it, which makes "who do I trust with my site, and who do
I report a bug to" unanswerable.

## What you may do

- Run this software, modified or not, for yourself or your company
- Fork it, publish your fork, and offer it as a service — subject to AGPL §13,
  which requires you to offer your users the source
- State factually that your project is "based on CrawlProof" or "a fork of
  CrawlProof", including a link back here
- Keep the existing attribution and copyright notices, which the licence
  requires anyway

## What you may not do

- Name your fork or your service "CrawlProof", or a name close enough to be
  confused with it
- Use the CrawlProof logo or visual identity as the branding for your fork
- Imply that your fork is the official CrawlProof, endorsed by it, or supported
  by Profullstack, Inc.

## Practically

If you fork this to run it yourself, replace the branding: the name in
`package.json`, the logo assets under `public/`, and the marketing copy under
`app/(marketing)/`. Nothing in the audit engine, the scanners, or the API
depends on the branding.
