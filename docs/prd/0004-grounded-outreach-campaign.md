## Problem

CrawlProof's Lead Generation feature is built but has zero awareness in the market. The target users — SaaS founders, agency owners, freelance consultants, and B2B sales reps — are currently paying for fragmented tool stacks (scraper + finder + sender) and do not know that CrawlProof offers a unified alternative. Without a deliberate go-to-market campaign, the feature will sit unused regardless of its quality. We need a low-cost, high-signal launch campaign that drives qualified signups, educates users on the "send from your own inbox" differentiator, and generates enough early usage data to iterate on the product.

## Goals

- Drive 1,000+ qualified landing-page visits to crawlproof.com/lead-generation within 30 days of campaign launch.
- Convert 10%+ of visitors into users who start at least one scrape.
- Generate 50+ public testimonials or case-study candidates from early users who book meetings or close deals via the feature.
- Establish "grounded outreach" and "send from your own inbox" as CrawlProof-owned positioning in the cold-email tooling space.
- Create reusable content assets (email sequences, social templates, landing-page copy) that the team can rerun for future feature launches.

## Non-Goals

- We will not run paid ads on Google or Meta in phase one. The campaign is organic-first to preserve margin and test messaging before scaling spend.
- We will not build a separate microsite or brand for the campaign. All traffic lands on crawlproof.com/lead-generation.
- We will not offer a lifetime deal or heavy discount. The feature is credit-based; we may offer bonus credits but not structural price cuts.
- We will not target enterprise sales teams (10+ seats) in this campaign. The messaging and UX are optimized for individuals and small teams.
- We will not promise specific reply-rate benchmarks in public marketing. We share aggregate data only after 30 days of real usage.

## Users

**Primary: SaaS Founders & Indie Hackers** — Active on Twitter/X, Indie Hackers, and Hacker News. They respect transparency, hate tool bloat, and will try a free tier if the value prop is clear. They are the most likely to share a positive experience publicly.

**Secondary: Agency Owners** — Active in Facebook groups, Slack communities, and LinkedIn. They care about time savings and ROI. A case study showing "10 hours of prospecting → 30 minutes" will resonate.

**Tertiary: Freelance Consultants** — Spread across Reddit (r/freelance, r/webdev), Discord servers, and niche newsletters. They are price-sensitive but loyal once a tool saves them money.

**Quaternary: B2B Sales Reps** — Active on LinkedIn and sales-specific communities. They need social proof from peers before switching tools.

## Requirements

- R1 [P0] Publish a dedicated landing page at crawlproof.com/lead-generation with hero copy, 3-step how-it-works, use-case cards, and a CTA to start a free scrape.
- R2 [P0] Write and schedule a 3-email sequence to existing CrawlProof users announcing the feature, showing a beta case study, and closing with a direct CTA.
- R3 [P0] Create a 2-minute demo video (screen recording + voiceover) showing scrape → enrich → send in one workflow, hosted on the landing page and shared on social.
- R4 [P0] Launch organic posts on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Indie Hackers with the core hook: "3 tools → 1 dashboard, and it sends from your own inbox."
- R5 [P1] Publish a long-form blog post / SEO article targeting keywords: "cold email from own domain," "directory scraper with email sender," "alternative to [competitor]."
- R6 [P1] Set up a "Rate My Sploof" style user-generated content loop: invite early users to share their reply rates and favorite pairings (directory + message angle) for a feature on the blog or social.
- R7 [P1] Create a simple referral mechanic: users who invite a friend to start a campaign both get 20 bonus credits.
- R8 [P2] Pitch the story to 5 newsletters or podcasts in the SaaS / indie-hacker space for earned media coverage.
- R9 [P2] Build a lightweight "Sploof Society" Discord or Slack community for power users to share tactics, pairings, and campaign results.
- R10 [P2] Design a one-page PDF "The Grounded Outreach Playbook" as a lead magnet for email capture from cold traffic.

## UX Notes

**Landing Page Structure**
- Hero: "Find leads. Verify them. Email them — from your own inbox." Subhead explains the 3-tool consolidation.
- Social proof bar: "Trusted by X founders, Y agencies, Z consultants" — populated from early beta users.
- 3-step visual: Scrape (directory URL) → Enrich (people + emails) → Send (your mailbox). Each step has a screenshot.
- "Why your own mailbox matters" section: short explainer on shared IP risk vs. personal domain reputation.
- Use-case cards: SaaS founder (Product Hunt scrape), Agency (local directory), Freelancer (Google search), Sales rep (Crunchbase).
- Pricing clarity: "No separate subscription. Uses your existing CrawlProof credits."
- CTA: "Start your first scrape — free" (leads to the in-app campaign builder).

**Email Sequence UX (for existing users)**
- Email 1: Announcement, soft CTA, sent to all active users.
- Email 2: Beta case study (47 leads, 6 replies, 2 demos), harder CTA, sent to non-converters from Email 1 after 3 days.
- Email 3: Final call, social proof aggregation, sent after 7 days.
- All emails plain-text preferred (aligns with the "from your own inbox" ethos) with one clear link.

**Social Post Templates**
- Twitter/X: Short, punchy, one visual (the 3-step diagram). Thread option for deeper dive.
- LinkedIn: Longer narrative post from a founder/team member's personal account, not the company page. Story-driven: "I used to pay for 3 tools..."
- Indie Hackers: Show HN-style launch post with demo video, asking for feedback rather than signups.

## Success Metrics

- 1,000+ unique visitors to /lead-generation in 30 days.
- 10%+ visitor-to-scrape conversion rate.
- 80%+ of started scrapes yield at least one enriched contact.
- 25%+ of enriched campaigns connect a mailbox and send.
- 8–12% reply rate on campaign emails within 14 days.
- 50+ organic social mentions or shares with the hashtag #GroundedOutreach or tag @crawlproof.
- 5+ inbound requests for case-study interviews or podcast appearances.
- Email sequence open rate >40%, click rate >10%.
- Referral mechanic drives 100+ new signups in 60 days.

## Risks & Open Questions

- **Messaging risk:** "Scrape + cold email" can read as spammy to platforms and moderators. We need to frame it as "grounded B2B outreach" and enforce an acceptable-use policy.
- **Competitor risk:** Larger players (Apollo, Hunter, Instantly) could copy the "send from your own inbox" angle quickly. Our moat is the unified dashboard + site audit context, not the feature alone. Are we messaging the audit-to-outreach connection clearly enough?
- **Community risk:** A public Discord/Slack of cold-email users could attract spammers or bad actors who damage the brand. Do we gate it behind a credit spend threshold?
- **Video production risk:** A low-quality demo video hurts more than no video. Should we hire a voice actor or keep it founder-narrated for authenticity?
- **Open question:** Should the landing page A/B test the hero headline ("3 tools → 1" vs. "send from your own inbox" vs. "grounded outreach") from day one, or pick one and iterate?
- **Open question:** Is the referral mechanic worth the engineering time for phase one, or should it be a fast follow after we validate demand?
